About this platform

This platform helps students preparing for the IBA DU admission test practice with full-length, realistically structured mock tests - 30 English, 25 Mathematics, and 15 Analytical questions per test, timed and scored exactly like the real exam's MCQ portion.

After every attempt, you get a detailed breakdown by section and topic, along with plain-language recommendations on what to revise next - generated by a rules-based engine, not guesswork.

This platform is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Institute of Business Administration, University of Dhaka.
